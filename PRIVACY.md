# Privacy Policy - Shopify Sales Exporter

**Last Updated**: January 3, 2026

## Overview

Shopify Sales Exporter ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

### From Your Shopify Store

The App requests read-only access to:
- **Orders**: To generate sales reports and calculate profits
- **Products**: To retrieve Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) information

We do NOT have access to:
- Customer payment information
- Customer personal data beyond what's in orders (email addresses)
- Your Shopify admin credentials
- Any write/edit permissions to your store

### Authentication Data

When you install the App, we store:
- Your shop domain (e.g., yourstore.myshopify.com)
- An OAuth access token (to access your store data)
- App session information

## How We Use Your Data

We use your data ONLY to:
1. Fetch orders and products when you request a report
2. Calculate revenue, COGS, and profit metrics
3. Display reports in the App interface
4. Generate CSV exports you download

## Data Storage

- **Authentication tokens**: Stored securely in our database (encrypted in production)
- **Orders and products**: NOT stored - fetched on-demand when you generate a report
- **Reports**: Generated in real-time, NOT saved on our servers
- **CSV exports**: Generated on-demand, sent directly to your browser

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your data to anyone
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising
- Store your sales or product data

Your data stays between you, your Shopify store, and this App.

## Data Security

- All connections use HTTPS encryption
- Access tokens are stored securely
- We follow Shopify's security best practices
- Regular security updates and patches

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Access**: View what data we store by contacting us
- **Delete**: Uninstall the App to remove your access token
- **Export**: Download your reports as CSV anytime

## Uninstalling

When you uninstall the App:
- Your OAuth access token is deleted from our database
- We lose all access to your store
- Any session data is cleared

## Children's Privacy

This App is intended for business use only. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
